HOW TO PLAY

  1. Preview (10s): All cards will be revealed for 10 seconds to memorize
  2. Turn: Player 1 goes first, each player flips 2 cards per turn
  3. Match: Same animals = keep open + earn 1 point + play again
  4. Miss: Different animals = flip back after 1.2s + switch turn
  5. Timer: Each turn has maximum 20 seconds
  6. Goal: Find all pairs. Highest score wins!
